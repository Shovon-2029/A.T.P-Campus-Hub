Common Queries

How do I track my report?
Upon submission, you receive a unique 12-digit ticket hash. Use this in the "Track" tab to see status updates without logging in.
Can the college trace my IP?
No. We act as a proxy layer. The data the college receives is strictly the content of your report, nothing more.
What issues can I report?
Anything from infrastructure damage and harassment to academic grievances and safety hazards.
